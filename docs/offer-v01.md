## The offer

A focused diagnostic review for someone who has a working local service but is unsure why it cannot be reached consistently, how multiple home routers relate to the problem, or what evidence is needed before considering Internet access.

The review uses HomeRouterLab Neon on an authorized Windows computer and delivers:

- **A network snapshot:** observed interfaces, addressing, default route, gateway, and an optional bounded route trace, with unknowns identified.
- **A service check and readable report:** the result of probing the agreed service URL, an HTML explanation, and a JSON record for technical review.
- **A practical next-step plan:** the evidence still needed for WAN/NAT/IPv6 decisions, a file-integrity receipt, and a clear distinction between local reachability and externally tested reachability.

## Scope and boundaries

The initial engagement is diagnostic. It does not change router or firewall configuration, collect router passwords, enable public sharing, or maintain a persistent agent. Internet reachability is not promised. A local check cannot establish external reachability, and the tool does not bypass CGNAT.

Device-specific configuration work, any external test, and any paid service would require an agreed scope before action. No patent exclusivity, vendor endorsement, regulatory certification, security certification, or compatibility with every router is claimed.

## Acceptance

The buyer can inspect the reported observations, reproduce the stated local probe, open the HTML report, and verify that the delivered files match the receipt. Unknown router/WAN facts remain marked unknown. Recommendations identify their required evidence rather than asserting that publishing has succeeded.

A fee and delivery time would be agreed only after confirming the authorized device, the service to check, and the requested scope. This draft intentionally makes no pricing or market-demand claim.
